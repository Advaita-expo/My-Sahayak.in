Play Store Assets & Checklist

Required (must have before publishing):
- High-res app icon: 512x512 (PNG)
- Feature graphic: 1024x500 (PNG)
- At least 2-3 screenshots (phone) — recommended sizes: 1080x1920
- Short description (80 chars max)
- Full description (up to 4000 chars)
- Privacy policy URL (HTTPS)
- Contact email and phone (for store listing)

Optional but recommended:
- Promo video (YouTube link)
- Tablet screenshots
- Localization for other languages

Data & policy:
- Complete Data safety form in Play Console
- Content rating questionnaire
- Confirm app contains no disallowed content

Testing tracks:
- Internal testing (quick verification)
- Closed testing (beta testers)
- Open testing (public beta)
- Production rollout (staged rollout recommended)

Files in repo folder:
- My Sahayak.aab  (upload this to Play Console)
- My Sahayak.apk  (signed APK)
- signing.keystore (backup of signing key)
