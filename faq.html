Frequently Asked Questions

What services does My Sahayak offer?
We provide domestic staffing solutions including cooks, housekeepers, nannies, and elderly care staff. All our staff are trained and background-verified for your peace of mind.
How do I book a service?
You can book a service by visiting our 'Book Now' page, selecting your required service, and filling out the booking form. Our team will contact you to confirm your requirements and schedule.
Are any services free?
No, all our services are paid. We believe in fair wages for our staff and transparent pricing for our clients. You can view pricing details on the booking page or contact us for a quote.
How are staff selected and verified?
All staff undergo a thorough background check, skills assessment, and training before being placed with clients. We ensure only qualified and trustworthy individuals join our team.
How can I get support or resolve an issue?
You can reach our support team via the Contact page. We are committed to resolving any issues promptly and ensuring your satisfaction with our services.
